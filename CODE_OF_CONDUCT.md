# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Focus feedback on the code, not the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, abusive, or threatening language.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or trolling.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, ask participants to stop, or restrict access to the project when needed to keep the community healthy.
